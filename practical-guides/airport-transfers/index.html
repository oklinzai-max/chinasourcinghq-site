How to get from the airport into the city at Guangzhou Baiyun, Shenzhen Bao'an and Yiwu: which terminal you land at, metro and intercity rail, airport coaches, taxi costs and the timing that matters before a meeting.

Quick answer

Guangzhou Baiyun now handles passengers through T2 and T3 only. T2 has Metro Line 3; T3 connects by intercity rail, which reaches Pazhou in about 30 minutes. Shenzhen Bao'an uses Metro Line 11. Yiwu airport is small and close to the city, so a taxi is simplest. Allow 60–90 minutes from landing to hotel.

Who this guide is for: Buyers arriving for a fair, a market visit or a factory meeting, especially those landing in the evening.

The three arrivals that matter

Most sourcing trips start at one of three airports. Each has a different default answer, and the wrong default costs an hour.

Airport City Best default Watch out for
CAN — Guangzhou Baiyun Guangzhou Metro Line 3 from T2; intercity rail from T3 Two operating terminals, different transport for each
SZX — Shenzhen Bao’an Shenzhen Metro Line 11 Terminal 3 is the main international terminal
YIW — Yiwu Yiwu Taxi Small airport, limited flight options

If you are heading to Yiwu via Hangzhou (HGH), you add a rail leg: airport to Hangzhou East station, then a high-speed train. That route makes sense when HGH has a much better connection than YIW, and not when you land late at night.

Guangzhou Baiyun: the terminal decides your route

Baiyun is no longer a two-terminal airport in the old sense. T1 stopped handling passengers in 2026, and traffic now runs through T2 and T3. The two are several kilometres apart and have different rail connections, so the first thing to do is check your boarding pass.

Terminal Metro Intercity rail Typical airlines
T2 Line 3, Airport North station, directly below No station at T2 China Southern, Cathay Pacific, Sichuan Airlines and partners
T3 No metro; shuttle to Gaozeng station Baiyun Airport East station, level B2, 3–5 minutes from arrivals Most international flights, Air China, Hainan Airlines

From T2: Metro Line 3

Line 3 runs from Airport North into the city. For the Canton Fair complex, change to Line 8 and alight at Pazhou. Allow 60–75 minutes door to door, including the change, the security queue and the walk into the halls.

Practical points:

  • Follow the bilingual Metro signs down to B1; do not exit the building looking for it.
  • You need a ticket or QR code before the gates. Foreign cards are not reliably accepted at machines — set up the metro mini-programme in Alipay or WeChat first.
  • Trains run from about 06:00 to 23:15 at the airport end, every few minutes at peak times.
  • Large suitcases on a packed train at 09:00 are unpleasant — consider a taxi on fair opening mornings.

Fares to the city centre are in the region of ¥7–9. Check the current fare on the metro map rather than relying on a blog figure.

From T3: intercity rail (usually the fastest)

The East Ring intercity line leaves from Baiyun Airport East station on level B2 of the T3 transport centre. It reaches Pazhou in about 29 minutes and Guangzhou South high-speed station in about 28 minutes — faster than the metro and unaffected by traffic.

  • Trains run roughly every 14 minutes.
  • Fares depend on destination, roughly ¥5–23.
  • Last trains leave around 21:43–22:41, so this is not an option for very late arrivals.
  • Buy at the station counter or on the 12306 app; a passport works for booking.

From Pazhou station it is a short walk to halls in areas B and C; for areas A and D, take Line 8 one stop to Xingang East.

If your flight lands too late for the intercity, take the free shuttle from the T3 transport centre to Gaozeng station (about 15 minutes), then Line 3 or Line 9 southbound.

Moving between T2 and T3

  • Free shuttle bus, 24 hours, roughly every 5–10 minutes at peak, about 20–25 minutes end to end.
  • Intercity train, about 6 minutes and around ¥5, if the timing suits.

Allow at least an hour if you have to change terminals with luggage.

Airport coaches

Coaches leave from the transport centres at both terminals and are the practical choice with oversized bags, because they have undercarriage storage and no changes.

Route Destination Rough fare Rough time
Line 1 Guangzhou Railway Station via Garden Hotel and Tianhe ¥22 About 60 minutes
Line 2 Yuexiu hotel district (Garden Hotel, Huanshi Dong Lu) ¥23 50–60 minutes; night service roughly 23:00–03:00
Line 3 Rosedale Hotel via Sanyuanli and Jiefang Road ¥26 About 50 minutes

Routes and fares change; confirm at the counter. Coaches are subject to traffic, including fair-period congestion around Pazhou.

The Canton Fair express coach

During fair periods Baiyun runs a direct coach to the Pazhou complex — no change, luggage handled, about 50 minutes.

  • Fare: around ¥35 with a Canton Fair badge or related document.
  • Airport to Pazhou: first departure about 09:00, last about 16:00.
  • Pazhou to airport: first about 11:00, last about 18:00.
  • Frequency: roughly every 30 minutes, with rolling departures when demand is high.
  • Boarding: the west coach station at T2 or T3. Buy at the counter, the airport coach mini-programme, or a self-service machine.
  • Exception: on the last day of each phase, the airport-to-Pazhou direction may not run. The return direction normally does.

This is the single easiest option if you are heading straight to the fair with luggage.

Taxi and ride-hailing

Use the official rank outside arrivals — ignore anyone approaching you inside the terminal.

  • To central Guangzhou: roughly ¥100–170 on the meter, plus ¥15–25 in tolls.
  • To Pazhou: at the upper end of that range, longer in fair traffic.
  • Have your destination written in Chinese; many drivers do not read English addresses.
  • Ride-hailing works, but pick-up points at large terminals can be confusing. Follow the app’s pin.

Shenzhen Bao’an (SZX)

Metro Line 11 is the fast link towards Futian and the city centre, with fewer stops than a typical urban line. It is the default for most buyers.

Taxi is reasonable for Bao’an hotels or factory visits in the west of the city, where the metro does not reach.

Note that Shenzhen is a long city: the airport is in the west, while Huaqiangbei is in Futian, and factories are spread across Bao’an, Longgang and Guangming. Check where your first meeting is before choosing a hotel.

Yiwu (YIW)

Yiwu airport is small and close to the city — the transfer is short and the terminal is easy to navigate. The limitation is route coverage: connections are strongest via major Chinese hubs.

If you fly via a hub:

  • Check whether baggage is through-checked.
  • Leave a generous connection time; domestic connections can involve a terminal change.
  • Do not book a tight connection before a market morning.

Pre-booked transfers: when they help

A pre-booked transfer is worth considering when:

  • you land after midnight;
  • you carry samples and do not want to negotiate a taxi;
  • you are travelling with colleagues and need one vehicle;
  • your hotel is hard to find or has no English name.

What to check before booking: the meeting point inside the terminal, whether the driver tracks your flight, whether the price is fixed, and what happens if your flight is delayed.

Timing rules

  • 60–90 minutes from landing to hotel, including immigration and baggage.
  • Two hours before a domestic connection.
  • Never schedule a supplier meeting within two hours of an international arrival.
  • Check the last metro or intercity train if you land in the evening — the T3 intercity stops earlier than the metro.

Payments at the airport

Set up mobile payment before you fly. Arrivals halls are where tired travellers discover their card does not work. A small amount of RMB cash is a useful backup, not a strategy.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming you land at T1. It no longer handles passengers; check your booking.
  • Planning a metro route from T3. There is no metro station at T3 — use the intercity or the shuttle.
  • Taking a taxi on a fair opening morning and sitting in traffic while the metro runs normally.
  • Landing without data and no offline map.
  • Using an unlicensed driver who approaches you inside the terminal.
  • Booking a tight connection through a Chinese hub without checking terminal changes.

Frequently asked questions

Which Guangzhou terminal will I land at?

Baiyun now handles passengers through T2 and T3 only; T1 stopped passenger operations in 2026. T2 is used by China Southern, Cathay Pacific and Sichuan Airlines among others; most international flights use T3. Check your booking — the terminal changes your ground transport completely.

Should I take a taxi or the metro from Guangzhou airport?

Metro from T2 if you have manageable luggage and arrive outside rush hour: it is predictable and cheap. Taxi if you land late, carry samples, or are heading somewhere not close to a metro line. From T3, intercity rail is usually faster than either.

How long does it take from CAN to the Canton Fair complex?

About 30–35 minutes by intercity rail from T3 to Pazhou, 60–75 minutes by metro from T2 including the change and the walk in, and roughly 50 minutes by the fair express coach. Opening mornings are slower by road.

Is Yiwu airport close to the market?

Yes — Yiwu airport is small and close to the city, which is why many buyers prefer it when a suitable flight exists. Confirm the transfer time with your hotel rather than assuming.

Do I need cash for the airport?

A small amount helps, but mobile payment covers most situations. Set up your payment app before you fly rather than at the arrivals hall.
