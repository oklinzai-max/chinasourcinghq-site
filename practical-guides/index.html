Practical essentials

Prepare for Your China Business Trip

The practical side of a sourcing trip: intercity transport, payments and mobile data, entry rules, talking to suppliers, and moving samples and orders out of China.

Good to know: Entry rules, transport schedules and payment limits change often. Each guide carries a verification date and marks anything we could not confirm.

None of this is glamorous, and all of it decides whether the trip works. Each guide is written to be used before you travel, not read on the plane.

Start with the question you actually have

Do I need a visa? How do I pay in China? How do I book a train? What should I pack?

Before you go

Practical Visa & Invitation Letter for China A practical starting point for China entry rules: business visa basics, when an invitation letter helps, how invitation letters are issued, and why the rules depend on your nationality and change often. Preparation China Business Trip: Documents & Packing Checklist What to prepare before a China sourcing trip: documents and paperwork, money and payment setup, connectivity, a sourcing kit that actually gets used, and a seven-day countdown.

Getting around

Practical Getting Between Cities in China How to move between Chinese sourcing cities: booking high-speed rail with a passport, airport procedures, ride-hailing and metro payments, plus the buffer times that prevent missed connections. Transport Booking China High-Speed Trains as a Foreigner How to buy China high-speed rail tickets with a foreign passport: the official 12306 route, what happens at the station, seat classes explained, and when a booking platform is worth the fee. Transport Airport to City: Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Yiwu How to get from the airport into the city at Guangzhou Baiyun, Shenzhen Bao'an and Yiwu: which terminal you land at, metro and intercity rail, airport coaches, taxi costs and the timing that matters before a meeting.

Money and data

Practical Payment & Connectivity in China How international buyers handle money and mobile data in China: linking foreign cards to mobile payment apps, cash and ATMs, eSIM options, and which services may not work on local networks.

Working with suppliers

Practical Business Communication with Chinese Suppliers Practical communication habits for working with Chinese suppliers: first contact, WeChat etiquette, how to read a quotation, negotiation norms, and how to confirm details in writing. Reference China Sourcing Terms: MOQ, FOB, CIF, DDP and the Rest A plain-English glossary of the trade terms that decide whether two supplier quotes are actually comparable: Incoterms, MOQ, tooling, lead time, AQL, HS codes and shipping documents.

Moving samples and goods

Practical Samples, Shipping & Logistics How samples and production orders move from China to your market: courier versus freight forwarder, consolidation, Incoterms explained, customs duties, and what to confirm before anything ships.
