This policy explains what happens to information when you visit ChinaSourcing. It is written to be read, not to be survived. If something here is unclear, ask us.

The short version

  • You do not need an account, and there is no login.
  • Downloading a template does not require an email address.
  • No analytics script is loaded while the site has no measurement ID configured.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data.
  • The newsletter form collects only your email address. Submitting it adds you to the list straight away — there is no separate confirmation email — and you can unsubscribe or ask us to delete it at any time. Brevo sends the emails on our behalf.

What we collect

Nothing you type into the tools. The cost calculator and trip planner run entirely in your browser. Their inputs are not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and they disappear when you close the page.

Nothing from downloads. The Excel and Word templates are ordinary static files. There is no registration wall, no email gate and no download tracking beyond the aggregate analytics described below.

Server logs. Whoever hosts this site may keep standard server logs — IP address, request time, page requested, browser type — for security and troubleshooting. We do not use them to identify individuals.

Analytics

The site is built to support Google Analytics 4, but no analytics script loads while the measurement ID is empty. In the current configuration, visiting this site makes no request to Google.

If a measurement ID is added later, GA4 would collect:

  • Pages viewed and approximate time on page
  • Approximate location derived from IP address, with IP anonymisation enabled
  • Device, browser and operating system
  • Referral source, including search engines
  • Interaction events such as affiliate link clicks and template downloads

Those events record a link ID, a provider name, a category and the page the click happened on. They do not record your name, email address or anything you type into the tools.

If analytics is enabled, you can block it with your browser's tracking protection or a content blocker. We do not attempt to detect or defeat blockers.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies for advertising or profiling. Google Analytics, if enabled, sets its own cookies; their names and lifetimes are documented by Google. Third-party sites you reach through an outbound link set their own cookies under their own policies.

Email subscription

What we collect. Only the email address you type into the form, plus the record of when and where you subscribed and your current subscription status. We do not ask for your name, company, phone number or country, and we do not enrich the address with data from anywhere else.

Why we collect it. To send the two documents the form promises — the Supplier Comparison Spreadsheet and the Common Chinese-English Sourcing Terms — and occasional sourcing insights, market updates and practical buyer resources. Nothing else. We do not use the address for advertising, retargeting, profiling or resale.

Who processes it. Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) sends these emails on our behalf and stores the address and the subscription record in the European Union. Brevo acts as our data processor under a data processing agreement; its own privacy policy is at brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy . We do not run our own mail server and we never see your address outside Brevo's dashboard.

No confirmation step. Submitting the form adds your address to the list immediately. We do not send a separate confirmation email and we do not ask you to click a link before the resources arrive. The two documents are delivered automatically as email attachments, in two emails, each carrying one file.

Record of consent. When you submit the form, Brevo records the date and time, the form or page the request came from, and the current subscription status. We keep that record for as long as you remain subscribed, because it is the evidence that you asked for these emails.

Unsubscribing. Every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe link in the footer. It takes effect immediately and without a reason being asked for. Once you unsubscribe, no further marketing email is sent. You can also reply to any email and ask to be removed. We keep a minimal suppression record so that you are not added again by accident.

Deleting your data. Write to us through the contact page and ask for deletion. We remove the address from Brevo and confirm by email, normally well inside 30 days. Deletion is separate from unsubscribing: unsubscribing stops the emails, deletion removes the record.

What we will not do. We do not buy, rent or import contact lists, we do not add people who merely downloaded a free template or sent a question through the contact form, and we do not pass your address to any third party for their own use.

Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, you leave this site and the partner's own privacy policy applies. The partner may set cookies to attribute the booking. We do not receive your name, payment details or booking contents; we may receive aggregate commission reports.

As of the last update, no affiliate programme has approved this site, so no affiliate links are active. See the affiliate disclosure for live status.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to answer it and to correct the content you wrote about. We do not add correspondents to a mailing list.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to processing. Since we collect almost nothing, most requests will result in a short answer — but ask anyway.

Contact: contact page. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is aimed at business buyers and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes materially — for example when analytics or a mailing service is enabled — the date at the top will change and the change will be described here rather than quietly applied.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact page. Site publisher: ChinaSourcing.

This page describes current practice. It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a review by a qualified professional in your jurisdiction before commercial launch.
