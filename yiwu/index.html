Zhejiang · Yiwu International Trade City

Yiwu Wholesale Market: Buyer Guides

How to source in Yiwu: the district and floor layout of Yiwu International Trade City, a realistic market day, where to stay, and how to negotiate without wasting the trip.

Good to know: Floor and district details are condensed from official market listings. Booth-level ranges change, so confirm your category before travelling.

Yiwu is one market made of several enormous districts, and each district groups products by floor. Decide which district covers your category before you arrive — everything else follows from that one decision.

Market overview

The market is built for small commodities and mixed, low-MOQ orders. It is not a single hall you can walk end to end in a morning: a productive visit means choosing a small number of districts and treating each as its own sourcing trip.

On the ground Yiwu: Inside the Market An hour-by-hour picture of sourcing inside Yiwu International Trade City: how booths work, what to bring, how to collect quotes, and how to leave with usable supplier contacts.

Districts and floors

Products are grouped by district and then by floor. The gate signs carry the floor directory, which is the fastest way to reject the wrong building before you spend an hour inside it.

The market guide walks through how the districts and floors are organised, and what to check against the official listings before you travel.

Product categories

Yiwu's strength is variety at low minimums: small goods, accessories, household items and anything sold in large assortments. Heavier categories — furniture, machinery, electronics at scale — usually belong in a different city.

Buying tips

A booth can look identical to the one opposite. The difference is usually in minimums, packaging, and whether the price includes an export service. Write those three things down at every stop.

Transport and hotels

Yiwu has its own station and airport access, but most sourcing trips arrive from Guangzhou or Shanghai. Staying near the market saves the commute; staying near the station saves the transfer. Choose based on which one you do more often.

Common questions

Which district sells my product?

Start with the district and floor guide. Booth ranges change, so use it to shortlist the right building, then confirm the specific category with the market listings before travelling.

How many days do I need?

The itinerary guide builds a realistic market day around a short list of districts. Two full days is usually more useful than one rushed day across the whole complex.

Can I pay by card at the stalls?

Mobile payment is the normal route. Sort out connectivity and payment before you land; the payment and connectivity guide explains what to set up.
