Yiwu: Arrival & Where to Stay

How to reach Yiwu from Guangzhou, Shanghai or Hangzhou, and how to choose a hotel near Yiwu International Trade City based on which district you plan to work.

Quick answer

Most buyers reach Yiwu by high-speed train from Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Shanghai or Hangzhou, or fly into Yiwu (YIW) or Hangzhou (HGH). Stay within walking distance of the district you will work — Districts 1 to 4 sit close together, while District 5 and District 6 require a different base or a taxi.

Airport
YIW (Yiwu) — small, close to the city
Alternative airport
HGH (Hangzhou) — then high-speed rail to Yiwu
Rail
Yiwu railway station, high-speed services from major cities
Hotel logic
Base near the district you will work, not the city centre

Who this guide is for: Buyers planning their first Yiwu trip and choosing between flying, taking the train and picking a hotel area.

Three ways in

1. Fly to Yiwu (YIW)

The most convenient option when it works. Yiwu airport is small and close to the city, so the transfer is short and predictable. The limitation is route coverage: connections are strongest via major Chinese hubs.

If you fly via a hub, check whether your baggage is through-checked and leave a generous connection time. Domestic connections in China can involve a terminal change.

2. Fly to Hangzhou (HGH), then take the train

Hangzhou has far more international and domestic connections. The trade-off is an extra leg:

  • Airport to Hangzhou East railway station — metro or taxi, roughly 40–60 minutes.
  • Hangzhou East to Yiwu by high-speed train — typically around an hour, but check the current timetable.
  • Yiwu station to your hotel — 20–30 minutes by taxi.

This route works well if you are already in Shanghai or Hangzhou, and poorly if you land late at night with luggage.

3. High-speed rail from Guangzhou or Shenzhen

This is the standard option for buyers combining the Canton Fair with Yiwu. Direct services exist, but the timetable is counter-intuitive: the early morning train takes about 8h 30m, while the mid-afternoon one takes about 5h 20m. Choose based on whether you want to arrive with daylight or spend fewer hours on board. See the Guangzhou to Yiwu route guide for the comparison between flying and rail.

Booking notes:

  • Your passport is your ticket. The name and number must match exactly.
  • Book with enough buffer to reach the station — Chinese high-speed stations are large and security queues are real.
  • Second class is usually fine. First class is quieter if you need to work.

Where to stay: match the hotel to the district

Yiwu’s hotel geography is simpler than Guangzhou’s, but the district you plan to work still matters.

Area Best for Trade-offs
Around Districts 1–4 Walking to the main market complex, early starts Busy at trade-event periods; rooms book out
Near District 5 Home textiles, imported goods, auto parts buyers Further from the main cluster
Near District 6 (Global Digital Trade Center) Smart devices, trend toys, fashion jewellery Newer area; fewer established restaurants
City centre (Binwang) Evening restaurants, gyms, slightly lower rates Daily commute to the market
Near the railway station Early departures, one-night stops Least convenient for sourcing

Practical hotel checks

  • Walking distance to the specific district entrance, not just “near the market”.
  • Foreign guest registration — international hotels handle this routinely; small guesthouses may not.
  • Breakfast start time — a 7:00 breakfast matters if you want to be at the market by 8:30.
  • Laundry — useful on a multi-city trip; ask about turnaround time.
  • A desk and reliable Wi-Fi — you will spend evenings writing quotations.
  • Luggage storage — helpful if you check out before a late train.

Getting around Yiwu

  • Taxi and ride-hailing are cheap and the default for station transfers and district-to-district moves.
  • Walking works within the District 1–4 cluster.
  • Hotel shuttle buses exist at some properties; confirm routes and times rather than assuming.
  • Have addresses in Chinese. Drivers may not read English hotel names.

Arrival-day checklist

  1. Change or withdraw a small amount of cash for incidentals.
  2. Get a data connection working before you leave the airport or station.
  3. Save your hotel address in Chinese on your phone.
  4. Buy water and a power adapter if needed.
  5. Walk to the market entrance once in the evening so the morning is not a navigation exercise.
  6. Confirm the opening time of the district you plan to visit.

Money and connectivity in Yiwu

Yiwu is more cash-friendly than major Chinese cities in some market contexts, but mobile payment dominates. Set up at least one working payment method before arrival — see payment and connectivity for the practical steps and the limits to check.

Common mistakes

  • Booking a hotel “near Yiwu” that is actually a 20-minute drive from the market.
  • Arriving late and planning to “figure out the market tomorrow” — the first morning is your most valuable slot.
  • Flying into Hangzhou without accounting for the station transfer and missing the last convenient train.
  • Assuming there will be an airport shuttle to your specific hotel.
  • Not checking whether your stay overlaps a public holiday, when booths and services may close.

Before you book

Hotels near Yiwu International Trade City

  • Compare the total price including taxes and fees, not the nightly headline rate.
  • Check the cancellation window — plans change often around trade fairs.
  • Look at the walk to the nearest metro station, not just the district name.

Frequently asked questions

Is it better to fly into Yiwu or Hangzhou?

Fly into YIW if there is a convenient connection, because the airport is close to the city. Hangzhou often has more international connections, but you then add a high-speed train leg of roughly one to one and a half hours plus transfers.

How far is Yiwu railway station from the market?

Roughly 20–30 minutes by taxi depending on traffic. Many hotels near the market are a short taxi ride from the station.

Should I stay near the market or downtown?

Near the market if sourcing is the purpose of the trip. Downtown Yiwu has more restaurants and evening options but adds a daily commute.

Can I walk between districts?

Districts 1 to 4 are close enough that walking between them is realistic. District 5 and District 6 are further away and better reached by taxi or shuttle.
