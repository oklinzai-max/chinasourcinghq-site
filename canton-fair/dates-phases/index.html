Verified Canton Fair dates for the 141st session in spring 2027, what each of the three phases covers, and how to choose the phase that matches your product category.

Quick answer

The 141st Canton Fair runs 15 April – 5 May 2027 in three phases: Phase 1 (15–19 April) for electronics, machinery, hardware, vehicles and lighting; Phase 2 (23–27 April) for houseware, gifts, building materials and furniture; Phase 3 (1–5 May) for textiles, apparel, shoes, bags, office supplies and food.

Who this guide is for: Buyers booking flights and hotels who need to know which five-day window to attend.

The 141st session at a glance

Phase Dates Length Product focus
Phase 1 15–19 April 2027 5 days Home appliances, consumer electronics, industrial automation, machinery, power equipment, vehicles and parts, motorcycles, bicycles, lighting, electronic and electrical products, new energy, hardware, tools
Phase 2 23–27 April 2027 5 days Daily ceramics, kitchenware, houseware, glassware, home decoration, garden products, festival and gift items, watches and optical products, art ceramics, woven and rattan crafts, building and decorative materials, sanitaryware, furniture
Phase 3 1–5 May 2027 5 days Toys, baby and children’s products, apparel, underwear, sportswear, fur and down, fashion accessories, fabrics, shoes, luggage and bags, home textiles, carpets, office supplies, health and medical devices, food, sports and leisure, personal care, bathroom products, pet products

The product ranges above are translated and condensed from the official phase list. The official site publishes the full section-by-section breakdown, and it is worth checking it against your own product before you commit to a phase.

Where each phase sits in the complex

Once you have picked a phase, the next question is which area to enter. The complex has four areas (A–D), and each phase distributes its sections across them:

Phase Area A Area B Area C Area D
1 Electronics, appliances, machinery, new energy vehicles Hardware, tools, vehicle parts Motorcycles, bicycles, lighting, electrical products Power equipment, processing machinery, industrial automation, service robots
2 Kitchenware, ceramics, garden, festival, rattan Furniture, building materials, sanitaryware Houseware, glass artware Home decor, gifts, watches, fashion jewellery
3 Apparel, shoes, fashion accessories Food, office supplies, sports and leisure, medical Home textiles, fabrics Toys, baby products, bags and luggage

Two practical consequences:

  • Enter by the station that matches your area. Pazhou station serves areas B and C; Xingangdong serves A and D. See the transportation guide.
  • Some phases are concentrated in one area. Phase 3 apparel buyers can spend the whole day in Area A. Phase 1 electronics buyers will cross A and D.

The full hall-by-hall table is in the complete buyer guide.

Why the phase choice matters more than the dates

Three decisions follow directly from the phase you pick:

Flight timing. Phase 1 opens the spring cycle, so arrivals on 13–15 April are the busiest. If you fly into Guangzhou on the morning of 15 April, expect long immigration queues and heavy traffic towards Pazhou.

Hotel pricing. Rates in Guangzhou typically rise across the whole fair period, with the steepest premiums around each phase opening. Booking outside the phase you attend can be surprisingly cheaper — as long as you accept the commute.

What you can do in the gaps. The three-day gaps are a gift for buyers with more than one category. A common pattern is Phase 1 plus three days for a Shenzhen or Zhongshan factory visit, or Phase 2 plus a Yiwu trip.

How to read the product list correctly

The phase list uses exhibition categories, not retail categories. A few translations that catch buyers out:

  • “Lighting products” is in Phase 1, not Phase 2, even though decorative home products are in Phase 2.
  • Furniture sits in Phase 2 alongside building materials — useful if you source both for a project.
  • Bags, shoes and textiles are Phase 3, together with office supplies and food.
  • Toys and baby products are Phase 3, not Phase 1 as many first-time buyers assume.

If your product appears in two phases, check which sections the specific exhibitors you want are in. The exhibitor directory is searchable by company name once the fair publishes it.

What about the autumn session?

The autumn edition follows the same three-phase structure, usually starting in mid-October and finishing in early November. The 140th session ran 15 October – 4 November 2026, with phases on 15–19 October, 23–27 October and 31 October – 4 November.

We do not publish future autumn dates until the organiser confirms them. Anything else would be a guess dressed up as a schedule.

Planning backwards from your phase

A workable timeline for a spring 2027 visit:

  • 12 weeks out — confirm your phase, check visa or visa-free eligibility for your passport, and block the dates.
  • 10 weeks out — register interest for a buyer badge and start watching hotel rates.
  • 8 weeks out — book flights and accommodation; request any invitation letter your registration requires.
  • 4 weeks out — build your exhibitor target list with booth numbers; prepare a one-page company profile.
  • 1 week out — download offline maps, set up payment and data, print your badge confirmation.
  • Day before — collect your badge if early collection is available, and walk the metro route once.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the fair is open to the public. It is a trade event with buyer registration; plan for it.
  • Booking a hotel for the wrong phase. Check-in dates that overlap a phase boundary are the most expensive and the least useful.
  • Leaving the gaps unplanned. Three free days in Guangzhou with no appointments is three wasted days.
  • Trusting a third-party dates page. Secondary sources copy each other and go stale. Use the official site and note the date you checked.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a gap between the phases?

Yes. Phase 1 ends on 19 April and Phase 2 begins on 23 April, so there are three days in between. Phase 2 ends on 27 April and Phase 3 starts on 1 May, again with three days in between. Those gaps are useful for factory visits or a short trip to Yiwu.

How long is each phase?

Each phase runs for five days including the opening day. Plan to arrive the day before if you want a full first morning.

Are the dates the same every year?

The pattern is stable — mid-April to early May in spring, mid-October to early November in autumn — but the exact dates shift. Always confirm on the official site before booking anything non-refundable.

Can I attend two phases in one trip?

Yes, if your badge and travel plans allow it. The trade-off is cost and time: staying for two phases usually means 10–14 nights in Guangzhou, with three idle days in between.
