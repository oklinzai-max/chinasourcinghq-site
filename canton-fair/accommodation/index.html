Canton Fair Accommodation Guide

How to choose a hotel for the Canton Fair in Guangzhou: the trade-offs between Pazhou, Tianhe, Haizhu, Liwan and airport-side areas, and what to check before you book during fair dates.

Quick answer

Stay on or near Pazhou if your priority is walking to the halls and you can accept peak pricing. Stay along Metro Line 8 in Tianhe or Haizhu for a 20–40 minute commute at lower cost. Airport-side hotels only make sense for very short trips.

Closest area
Pazhou (fair complex), Haizhu District
Best value commute
Metro Line 8 corridor — Tianhe, Haizhu
Airport
CAN, Baiyun District — far from the venue

Fair phases, especially opening nights

Who this guide is for: Buyers booking accommodation for a five-day phase, especially first-time visitors who do not know Guangzhou.

The one decision that shapes your week

Guangzhou is large, and the fair complex sits in Pazhou, on the south bank of the Pearl River. Where you sleep determines whether you spend 10 minutes or 90 minutes getting to your first meeting each morning.

There are four realistic options, and each is a different trade-off rather than a different quality level.

Area Nearest metro Route to the halls Rough door-to-door
Pazhou / fair complex Pazhou (Line 8) Walk, or one stop on Line 8 5–15 minutes
Tianhe / Zhujiang New Town Tiyu Xilu (Line 3), Zhujiang New Town (Line 3/5) Line 3 to Kecun, change to Line 8 25–35 minutes
Haizhu (outside Pazhou) Kecun, Lujiang or Zhongda (Line 8) Line 8 direct 15–30 minutes
Liwan / Yuexiu (old city) Gongyuanqian (Line 1/2), Haizhu Square (Line 2/6) Line 2 to Changgang, change to Line 8 35–50 minutes
Baiyun (airport side) Airport North (Line 3) Line 3 to Tiyu Xilu, then Line 8 via Kecun 70–90 minutes

Two things to know before you book:

  • Line 8 is the fair line. Pazhou, Xingang East and Modiesha all serve the complex, and which one you use depends on your hall — see the transportation guide.
  • Kecun and Changgang are the two interchanges you will use most. Kecun connects Line 3 and Line 8; Changgang connects Line 2 and Line 8. A hotel near either one shortens the trip noticeably.

All times assume a normal service day. During fair phases, trains are more frequent but also more crowded, and the last train still leaves around 23:00 — check the metro’s current first and last times before booking a late arrival.

Pazhou: pay for the walk

Staying within walking distance of the complex removes two metro rides a day, plus the security queue. During a five-day phase that is several hours of your life back — and it lets you drop purchases and catalogues at midday instead of carrying them.

What to check:

  • Actual walking distance to the hall you use. The complex is huge; the wrong side of Pazhou can still mean a 25-minute walk.
  • Minimum-stay rules. Some properties require five nights across peak fair dates.
  • Cancellation terms. Fair-period bookings are often non-refundable; if your plans might change, pay attention.

The Metro Line 8 corridor

Line 8 runs through the fair area and connects to the wider network. Hotels within a five-minute walk of a Line 8 station give you the best balance of price and predictability.

Two practical points:

  1. Peak crowding is real. At 9:00 and 18:00 during a phase, trains towards Pazhou and back are packed. Leave 20 minutes earlier than the journey time suggests.
  2. Station exits matter. Some exits are much closer to specific halls than others; check the map once you know your target section.

Old-city areas: markets on your doorstep

If your trip includes wholesale market visits in Liwan or Yuexiu, staying there for the market days and moving closer to Pazhou for the fair days is a legitimate strategy. It splits the trip into two bases, which is more work but often more efficient.

This works especially well for apparel, leather goods, shoes and small commodities buyers who plan to spend two or three days in the markets either side of their fair phase.

What not to do

  • Book on price alone. A cheap room 60 minutes away costs you two hours a day during the most expensive week of your sourcing year.
  • Trust a map pin without checking the river. Two hotels can look adjacent on a map and require a 30-minute detour to cross the Pearl River.
  • Assume a shuttle exists. Ask directly, and ask whether it runs at the times you need.
  • Book the night before arrival only. Fair-period rooms do not get cheaper at the last minute.

Practical booking checklist

Before you confirm any reservation:

  • Walking distance or metro time to your specific hall, checked on a map
  • Cancellation deadline and whether the rate is refundable
  • Minimum-stay requirement
  • Breakfast included — useful when you leave before 8:00
  • Whether the hotel can register foreign guests (standard for international hotels, less certain for small guesthouses)
  • A phone number you can reach in China, saved offline

Where this fits in the wider trip

If you are combining the fair with Yiwu, book Guangzhou for the fair days and a separate Yiwu hotel near the market for the second leg. Our Canton Fair + Yiwu itinerary sets out a realistic sequence, and the transportation guide covers the airport and metro details.

Common mistakes

  • Staying in Baiyun to be near the airport, then commuting over an hour each way for five days.
  • Ignoring the metro’s first and last trains when booking an early flight or a late arrival.
  • Assuming hotel breakfast starts before your departure — check the opening time.
  • Forgetting that the venue has multiple halls and choosing a hotel for the wrong entrance.

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?

For a spring phase, three to four months ahead is sensible. Fair-period inventory in Pazhou and along Line 8 sells out early, and the remaining rooms get expensive rather than better.

Is staying in Pazhou worth the premium?

If you are attending all five days and carrying catalogues and samples, walking back to your room at the end of the day is genuinely valuable. For a two-day visit, the premium is harder to justify.

Do hotels near the fair require a minimum stay?

Some do during peak fair nights. Check the cancellation policy and minimum-night rules before you pay — this is the single most common booking surprise for first-time buyers.

Should I stay near Guangzhou South railway station?

Only if you are leaving for another city immediately after the fair. It is convenient for high-speed rail but adds commute time to the venue.
