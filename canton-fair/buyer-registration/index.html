Canton Fair Buyer Registration

How overseas buyers register for the Canton Fair: the two registration routes, what to prepare, what to do at the badge counter, and which details must be confirmed on the official site.

Quick answer

Register through the official Canton Fair buyer channels before you travel, not through a paid third-party agent. You will need passport details, company information and proof that you buy for import or wholesale. On-site registration is usually possible but costs you hours during the opening days.

Who can register
Overseas buyers purchasing for import, wholesale or business use
Two routes
Pre-registration online, or on-site registration at the fair
Core documents
Passport, business card, company details
Badge validity
Session-specific — do not assume last year's badge still works

Who this guide is for: First-time buyers, and returning buyers who need to renew a badge for a new session.

Two ways in — and which one to use

Route 1: pre-register online. You submit your details before travelling, receive a confirmation, and collect your badge quickly on arrival. This is the option that protects your first day in the halls.

Route 2: register on site. Counters at the fair complex handle new buyers. It works, but during the first two days of a phase the queues can consume half a morning — exactly when exhibitors are freshest and halls are quietest.

If you have the choice, pre-register. If you cannot, arrive a day early and register before the opening morning.

What you will need

Prepare these before you start the form, because most online registration systems time out while you hunt for a file:

  • Passport — valid well beyond your travel dates, with the exact spelling of your name as printed.
  • Business card — showing your company name, your name and your role.
  • Company information — registered name, address, website, business type, and what you buy.
  • A business document — commonly a business licence, tax registration or a letter on company letterhead. Requirements vary by buyer type, so check the current list.
  • Your buying profile — product categories and typical order volumes. This helps the fair route you to the right sections and can matter for eligibility.

For returning buyers, have your previous buyer badge number or registration record ready. Re-registration is faster when the system can match you to an existing record.

The step-by-step sequence

  1. Confirm your session and phase using the dates and phases guide. Registering for the wrong phase is a common and expensive mistake.
  2. Open the official buyer registration channel from the Canton Fair website. Avoid look-alike sites that charge a “registration service fee”.
  3. Create an account with an email address you actually monitor. Confirmation and badge details arrive there.
  4. Complete the company and buyer profile accurately. Your name must match your passport exactly — middle names included or excluded consistently.
  5. Upload the requested documents in the specified format and size.
  6. Submit and save the confirmation as a PDF on your phone and as a printout.
  7. Check visa implications before booking flights. See the visa and invitation letter guide for why this step should not be skipped.
  8. Collect your badge at the fair complex or an announced off-site collection point, with your passport and confirmation.

At the badge counter

Bring the physical passport, not a photocopy. Expect:

  • A short verification of your identity and company details.
  • A photograph taken on the spot for the badge.
  • Issuance of a lanyard badge that must be worn visibly inside the complex.

If your details do not match, you will be sent to a service desk. That is why the spelling on the registration form matters more than it seems.

Badge rules worth knowing

  • Badges are session-specific, not phase-specific. One badge normally covers all three phases of the session you registered for, so attending two phases does not require two registrations. It does not carry over to the next session.
  • Badges are personal. Lending or borrowing a badge is a quick way to be refused entry.
  • Re-entry is normally allowed within the same day, but security checks apply each time.
  • Lost badges need to be replaced at a service desk; expect a fee or a verification process.

The invitation letter question

Some buyers need an invitation letter for a visa application. The fair and various official channels can provide invitation documents, but eligibility and turnaround vary.

Two warnings:

  1. An invitation letter is not a visa. It supports an application; it does not guarantee approval.
  2. Do not pay a stranger for an invitation. If a visa agent offers a “guaranteed” fair invitation, verify the route with the official fair channels first.

If your nationality currently benefits from visa-free entry or transit visa-free arrangements, you may not need an invitation at all — but confirm that business activities such as attending a trade fair are permitted under that entry type.

Common mistakes

  • Registering the week before. Approval is usually quick, but document problems are not.
  • Using a company email you cannot access abroad. Verification codes and confirmations get stuck.
  • Ignoring the name-matching rule. “Mohammed” vs “Muhammad” on a passport is a real problem at the counter.
  • Paying a broker for something the official channel provides. Check the official site first.
  • Assuming a badge covers all phases. Confirm what your registration actually includes.

Needs verification before you rely on it

  • — The exact document set for your buyer type and nationality (for example whether a business licence copy or a letter from your company is required).
  • — Whether registration is free for your category, and whether any deposit applies.
  • — Cut-off dates for online pre-registration ahead of each phase.
  • — Whether early badge collection is offered at locations outside the fair complex.

These points depend on policies, schedules or operators that change. Confirm them on the official source linked below before booking or travelling.

Frequently asked questions

Is buyer registration free?

Historically overseas buyer badges have been issued free of charge, but fees and deposits have changed for some buyer categories. Do not budget on the assumption that it is free — confirm on the official registration page for your session before you travel.

Can someone register on my behalf?

Some companies use an agent, and some trading partners will help with an invitation. The badge itself is tied to your passport, so you still need to attend in person with your own documents.

Do I need an invitation letter for the visa?

It depends on your nationality and the visa route you use. Many buyers now travel visa-free or on a transit visa-free stay, in which case a fair invitation is irrelevant. Check both the fair requirements and the current Chinese entry rules for your passport.

What if my registration is rejected?

Ask the official buyer service desk which document was insufficient. Incomplete company information and mismatched passport details are the most common causes. A same-day fix is often possible on site, but not guaranteed on the opening morning.
