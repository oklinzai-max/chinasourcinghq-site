How to reach the Canton Fair complex in Pazhou from Guangzhou Baiyun airport, Guangzhou South railway station and the city centre, with metro routes, taxi notes and timing advice.

Quick answer

The fair complex is served by Metro Line 8 (Xingangdong and Pazhou stations), which is the most reliable way to arrive during fair dates. From Baiyun airport, take Line 3 and change to Line 8 at Kecun. From Guangzhou South railway station, take Line 2 and change at Changgang.

Airport
Guangzhou Baiyun (CAN) — metro Line 3
High-speed rail
Guangzhou South — metro Line 2
Venue access
Metro Line 8 — Pazhou (areas B, C) / Xingangdong (areas A, D)
Peak risk
08:00–09:30 on phase opening days

Who this guide is for: Buyers arriving by air or high-speed rail who need a predictable route to the venue on a busy morning.

First, understand the geography

The Canton Fair complex sits in Pazhou, Haizhu District, on the south bank of the Pearl River. Three access points matter:

  • Metro Line 8 — the backbone of fair access, with stations serving the complex area.
  • The river crossings — bridges and tunnels connect Pazhou to Tianhe and the city centre; all of them slow down at peak times.
  • The airport and high-speed rail stations — both are well outside Pazhou and require a metro change or a long taxi ride.

Distances feel short on a map and take longer in reality during a fair phase.

Which Line 8 station for which area

This is the single most useful thing to know before you arrive: the two stations serve different halves of the complex.

Station Serves Notes
Pazhou Areas B and C A few minutes’ walk from the exit to the halls
Xingangdong Areas A and D One stop from Pazhou; also reachable on foot once inside the complex

Getting off at the wrong station costs 15–20 minutes of walking, which matters on an opening morning. Check which area and hall your products sit in using the hall table in the complete buyer guide, then pick the station.

Signage inside the stations points to the areas, but it is worth knowing your letter before you go underground — mobile data is unreliable in the crowded platforms.

From Guangzhou Baiyun Airport (CAN)

By metro (recommended for light luggage). Take Line 3 from the airport towards the city, change at Kecun to Line 8, and alight at the station closest to your target hall — typically Xingangdong or Pazhou. Allow 60–75 minutes including the walk and security check.

Practical notes:

  • The airport metro station is in the terminal complex; follow signs rather than exiting the building.
  • You need a ticket or a transport card before entering; ticket machines accept cash and some accept international cards, but do not rely on that — carry some cash or set up a metro QR code in advance.
  • Large suitcases on a packed Line 8 at 9:00 are unpleasant. If you land in the morning on an opening day, consider a taxi.

By taxi. Use the official taxi rank at the terminal. Journey time is typically 45–70 minutes outside peak traffic, longer on opening mornings. Have your destination written in Chinese; the fair complex name is widely recognised by drivers during the fair.

By airport express bus. Routes and stops change, and they are less useful for the fair than for city hotels. Check current routes before relying on this.

From Guangzhou South Railway Station

If you arrive on a high-speed train from Shenzhen, Hong Kong West Kowloon, Changsha or beyond:

  • Metro: Line 2 from Guangzhou South, change at Changgang to Line 8. Roughly 40–55 minutes to the complex area.
  • Taxi: 35–50 minutes depending on traffic, often longer in the afternoon.

Guangzhou South is enormous. Allow 15 minutes simply to exit the station and find the metro entrance.

Getting around during the fair

Metro is the default. It is cheap, frequent and unaffected by road traffic. The trade-offs are crowding and the walk from platform to hall.

Official shuttle buses have historically connected the complex with selected hotels and transport hubs during fair periods. Routes and timings are published per session — confirm on the official site rather than assuming last year’s route still runs.

Taxis and ride-hailing work, but pickup and drop-off zones near the complex are often restricted or congested. Ask your hotel where drivers actually stop, and allow extra time for the last 500 metres.

Walking is underrated inside Pazhou. Once you are in the complex, moving between halls on foot is usually faster than exiting to a road.

Timing that saves an hour

  • Leave 30 minutes earlier than the journey planner suggests on opening days.
  • Avoid 08:30–09:30 if you can; exhibitors are setting up and queues peak.
  • Leave the halls by 17:30 if you want a seat on the metro.
  • Build in a buffer between an airport arrival and any same-day appointment. A two-hour delay is common enough to plan for.

Payment and tickets

Metro fares are low, but the payment method matters. Options include single-journey tickets from machines, a stored-value transport card, and QR codes inside Chinese payment apps. International cards work in some places and not others.

Before you travel, set up at least one working payment method — see payment and connectivity.

Common mistakes

  • Taking a taxi on the opening morning and sitting in traffic while the metro runs normally.
  • Getting off at the wrong Line 8 station and walking 20 minutes to the right hall.
  • Forgetting the last-train time when leaving a late evening meeting.
  • Assuming the airport express bus stops at your hotel without checking the current route.
  • Carrying too much cash because card acceptance felt uncertain — set up mobile payment instead.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take from Baiyun airport to the fair?

Plan for around 60–75 minutes door to door by metro including the change at Kecun and the walk into the complex. By taxi, 45–70 minutes depending on traffic, and longer at peak times.

Is there a direct metro line to the fair?

Yes — Line 8 serves the Pazhou area. There is no single line from the airport, so you will change trains once.

Should I take a taxi from the airport?

A metered taxi from the official rank is fine if you have luggage and arrive outside rush hour. Use the official rank only, and insist on the meter.

Can I walk from my hotel in Pazhou?

Often yes. Check the specific hall entrance you need — the complex is very large and 'Pazhou' covers more than one approach.
