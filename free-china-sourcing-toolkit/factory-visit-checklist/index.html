Factory visit record

This is a buyer preparation checklist, not a professional factory audit report. It does not replace an independent inspection, a compliance assessment or legal advice.

Date and time
Factory name
Address
Buyer / company
Product(s) reviewed
Order value under consideration

01 Company information

  • Legal company name (as on the business licence)
  • Business licence number and registered address
  • Factory address — confirm it matches the licence
  • Contact person, role, phone, WeChat
  • Year established and number of employees
  • Main products and export markets
  • Manufacturer or trading company?

02 Documents to request

  • Copy of the business licence
  • Export licence or registration, if applicable
  • Certification relevant to your product and market
  • Test reports for the exact model you intend to order
  • Recent inspection reports, if any
  • Bank account details in the company name

03 Production equipment and capacity

  • Main machines and their condition
  • Number of production lines
  • Monthly capacity and current utilisation
  • Which steps are in-house and which are outsourced
  • Tooling and mould capability
  • Peak season constraints

04 Quality control

  • Dedicated QC person or team?
  • Incoming material checks
  • In-process checks and recorded results
  • Final inspection before packing
  • Defect rate and how it is measured
  • How customer complaints are handled

05 Sample consistency

  • Compare production sample with the approved sample
  • Ask to see three units of the same item side by side
  • Check colour, finish, dimensions and weight
  • Confirm which sample becomes the production reference

06 Packaging

  • Unit packaging and retail readiness
  • Inner box and carton dimensions
  • Carton strength and stacking
  • Packing area cleanliness and method
  • Labelling, barcodes and shipping marks

07 Delivery schedule

  • Lead time for the first order
  • Lead time for repeat orders
  • Production planning method
  • What happens when a material is delayed
  • Chinese New Year and holiday shutdown dates

08 Certifications and compliance

  • Which certifications apply to your product and market
  • Who holds the certificate — factory or trading company
  • Whether the report covers your exact model
  • Who pays for additional testing
  • Labelling and language requirements

09 Commercial terms to confirm

  • Unit price and currency
  • MOQ per model and per colour
  • Incoterm and named port
  • Payment terms and deposit percentage
  • Sample cost and whether it is credited
  • Warranty and remedy for defects

10 Risk signals to note

  • Address on the licence does not match the factory you are standing in
  • Cannot show a business licence, or shows a different company name
  • Refuses a video call or a visit, and only communicates through a marketplace chat
  • Prices far below every other quote on the same specification
  • Certificate shown is for a different model, or in another company name
  • Sample is excellent but production is outsourced to a different workshop
  • Pressure to pay a large deposit to an account that is not the company account
  • No written quotation, only a WeChat message with a number
  • Answers about lead time or capacity change between conversations
  • Unwilling to name a reference customer or show a shipping document

11 Communication notes

  • Main contact after the visit
  • Preferred channel — WeChat, email, both
  • Response time observed during your visit
  • Language and translation arrangements
  • Photos taken (with permission) and what they show
