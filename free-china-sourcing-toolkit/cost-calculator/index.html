Carton volume & chargeable weight

Forwarders quote on volume or weight, whichever is greater. Measure one carton in centimetres and enter how many cartons the order needs.

Volume per carton = L × W × H ÷ 1,000,000 (m³)

Chargeable weight = the greater of gross weight and volume × 167 kg

The 167 kg per m³ figure is the standard air-freight conversion (1 m³ ≈ 166.67 kg). Sea LCL rates and minimum charges are set by the forwarder — this calculator does not quote freight.

What this estimate cannot tell you

  • — Whether the supplier's price is competitive. That needs three comparable quotes.
  • — Your exact duty and tax liability. Only your customs authority or broker can confirm that.
  • — Hidden costs such as bank charges, demurrage, storage, or rework after inspection.
  • — The cost of a defect you discover after the goods arrive.

This is a planning estimate, not a customs valuation, quotation or tax advice.
