English Supplier Email Templates

Eleven messages for the situations that repeat in every sourcing relationship. Copy one, replace the bracketed fields, and keep it short. Vague enquiries get vague answers.

Download all eleven (.docx) Back to the toolkit
01 Initial inquiry (first contact) Before you ask for a price. It filters out trading companies that do not make the product, and gets you the right contact.

Subject: Introduction — [company], [country], looking for [product] 

Subject: Introduction — [company], [country], looking for [product]

Dear [Name],

I am [your name] from [company], a [retailer / importer / distributor] based in [country]. We buy [product category] and are looking for a manufacturer for [product].

Before we ask for prices, two questions:
1. Do you currently export to [country / region]? If so, which certifications do you already hold for that market?
2. Who should we speak to about a quotation and samples?

If this is within your product range, please send your catalogue and price list for [product], with the MOQ for each model.

Best regards,
[Your name]
[Company] — [website]
[Phone / WeChat]
02 Request a quotation A first enquiry with a new supplier. Replace every bracketed field.

Subject: Quotation request — [product] for [company], [country] 

Subject: Quotation request — [product] for [company], [country]

Dear [Name],

We are [company], a [retailer / importer / distributor] based in [country]. We are looking for a supplier of [product] and found your company through [source].

Please quote for the following:
- Product: [name and model]
- Material: [specification]
- Size / weight: [details]
- Colour: [details]
- Packaging: [unit packaging and carton requirements]
- Quantity: [quantity] units for the first order, with [annual volume] expected per year
- Destination: [city, country]

Please confirm:
1. Unit price at this quantity
2. MOQ per model and per colour
3. Sample cost and lead time
4. Production lead time
5. Payment terms
6. Certifications you can provide for [market]

Please quote [FOB / CIF / EXW] [port].

Best regards,
[Your name]
[Company] — [website]
[Phone / WeChat]
03 Ask about MOQ When a price is given but the minimum order is unclear, or too high for a trial.

Subject: MOQ question — [product] 

Subject: MOQ question — [product]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your quotation for [product].

Could you clarify the minimum order quantity?
- MOQ per model:
- MOQ per colour:
- MOQ per carton:

We would like to start with a smaller trial order of [quantity] units to test the market before committing to a larger volume. Is a mixed order possible, for example [number] colours at [quantity] units each?

If a lower MOQ carries a different unit price, please quote both options so we can compare.

Best regards,
[Your name]
04 Request product samples Before any production commitment. Always ask whether the sample cost is credited.

Subject: Sample request — [product], [quantity] units 

Subject: Sample request — [product], [quantity] units

Dear [Name],

We would like to order samples of [product] before placing a production order.

- Model / reference:
- Quantity: [number] units
- Colour / finish:
- Please include the retail packaging if available

Please confirm:
1. Sample cost per unit
2. Courier cost to [city, country]
3. Dispatch date
4. Whether the sample cost is credited against a future order

Please send the tracking number once dispatched.

Best regards,
[Your name]
05 Negotiate pricing Only after you have comparable quotes. Asking for a discount without a specification invites a worse product, not a better price.

Subject: Price discussion — [product], [quantity] units 

Subject: Price discussion — [product], [quantity] units

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your quotation of [price] per unit at [quantity] units.

We are comparing several suppliers for this specification and would like to work with you if the terms are competitive.

Could you let us know:
1. Your best price at [higher quantity] units
2. Whether the price changes if we accept your standard packaging
3. Whether a repeat order within [timeframe] would improve the price

We are not asking you to match a price we cannot show you. We are asking what is possible at a realistic volume for us.

Best regards,
[Your name]
06 Ask about lead time Before confirming an order, and again before Chinese New Year or peak season.

Subject: Lead time confirmation — [product] 

Subject: Lead time confirmation — [product]

Dear [Name],

Before we confirm the order, please confirm the production schedule:

- Lead time for the first order: [quantity] units
- Lead time for a repeat order of the same quantity
- Earliest possible shipment date if we confirm by [date]
- Any scheduled factory closures in the next [number] months

We need the goods to arrive before [date] for [reason]. If that is not possible, please tell us now rather than after the deposit is paid.

Best regards,
[Your name]
07 Confirm packaging Before production starts, not after. Packaging affects freight cost and damage claims.

Subject: Packaging confirmation — [product] 

Subject: Packaging confirmation — [product]

Dear [Name],

Please confirm the packaging specification for our order:

- Unit packaging: [polybag / colour box / blister]
- Unit dimensions and weight:
- Units per inner box:
- Units per carton:
- Carton dimensions and gross weight:
- Carton strength / grade:
- Shipping marks and barcode requirements:

Please send a photo of the packed carton before shipment so we can confirm it matches this specification.

Best regards,
[Your name]
08 Discuss payment terms When terms are unclear, or when a supplier asks for full payment upfront.

Subject: Payment terms — [product] order 

Subject: Payment terms — [product] order

Dear [Name],

We would like to confirm the payment terms before placing the order.

Our standard arrangement is [30% deposit on order confirmation, 70% against the bill of lading]. Could you confirm whether this works for you?

Please also confirm:
- The account name and currency for the deposit
- Whether the account is in the company name
- Which documents you provide before the balance is paid

For a first order we prefer to keep the balance until the goods are ready and inspected.

Best regards,
[Your name]
09 Follow up on an order At agreed milestones. Short and specific beats a vague chase.

Subject: Order status — [PO number], due [date] 

Subject: Order status — [PO number], due [date]

Dear [Name],

I am following up on order [PO number] for [product], confirmed on [date].

Could you confirm:
1. Production status (%)
2. Any material or schedule issues
3. Planned completion date
4. Planned shipment date and booking reference

If anything has changed since our last update, please tell me now so we can adjust our planning.

Best regards,
[Your name]
10 Report a quality problem Use with photos and facts. A clear complaint is easier to resolve than an angry one.

Subject: Quality issue — order [PO number], [product] 

Subject: Quality issue — order [PO number], [product]

Dear [Name],

We have received order [PO number] and found a quality problem that we need to resolve with you.

- Product: [name / model]
- Quantity affected: [number] of [total] units
- Problem: [description]
- Compared against: approved sample dated [date]

I have attached photographs showing the issue and the approved sample side by side.

Please let us know:
1. What you believe caused the difference
2. What you propose to do about the affected units
3. How you will prevent a recurrence in the next order

We would like to resolve this quickly and continue working together, so a clear proposal this week would help.

Best regards,
[Your name]
11 Customisation / OEM inquiry When you want your logo, colour, material or packaging changed. Ask what is free and what adds cost before you commit.

Subject: Customisation enquiry — [product], [logo / packaging / material] 

Subject: Customisation enquiry — [product], [logo / packaging / material]

Dear [Name],

We are interested in [product / model] and would like to discuss a customised version.

What we would like to change:
- Logo: [position, size, printing method]
- Colour: [Pantone reference or physical sample]
- Material: [current material → requested material]
- Packaging: [printed box / bag / insert / label]
- Size or specification: [details]

What must stay the same: [the parts that cannot change]

Please confirm:
1. MOQ for a customised version, per colour and per size
2. Any mould, tooling or printing-plate cost — one-off, or charged per order
3. Sample lead time, and the cost of a customised sample
4. Which changes are free and which add to the unit price
5. Whether you can send photos or video of a similar customised order you have produced

Best regards,
[Your name]

Before you hit send

  • — Replace every bracketed field. A template sent with [product] still in it is worse than no email.
  • — State the Incoterm you want. FOB, CIF and DDP prices are not comparable.
  • — Ask for the specification back in writing. That is where misunderstandings are caught.
  • — Keep the same thread. Forwarding a new email for every message loses the history.

General business correspondence, not legal advice. Adapt payment and contract terms to your own situation.
