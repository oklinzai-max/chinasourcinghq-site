A realistic two-city sourcing itinerary combining a Canton Fair phase with Yiwu International Trade City, including day-by-day sequencing, buffer days, and what to book when.

Quick answer

Attend your Canton Fair phase first, use the three-day gap between phases for travel and factory visits, then work Yiwu for two to three days before flying home from Yiwu or Hangzhou. Total: 10–13 days including arrival and departure buffers.

Suggested length
10–13 days
Route
Guangzhou (fair) → Yiwu (market) → departure
Fair phase
Choose by product — see dates and phases
Buffer
1 arrival day, 1 departure day minimum

Who this guide is for: Buyers sourcing both factory-made goods and small commodities, who want one trip instead of two.

Why this combination works

The Canton Fair and Yiwu do different jobs:

  • The fair is where you meet export-oriented factories, compare specifications and establish a price baseline.
  • Yiwu is where you find breadth: thousands of small consumer goods, low minimums, mixed orders, and fast-moving product variety.

Buyers who need both often try to do them in two separate trips. One well-sequenced trip is usually cheaper and produces better decisions, because you can compare a factory quote from the fair with a market quote in Yiwu within the same week.

The sequence

Guangzhou first, Yiwu second. Three reasons:

  1. The fair dates are fixed; Yiwu is flexible.
  2. Fair quotes give you a reference point for market pricing.
  3. Departing from Yiwu or Hangzhou is often easier at the end of a trip than returning to Guangzhou.

The exception: if your only goal is small commodities, skip the fair and go straight to Yiwu.

Day-by-day outline

This is a planning example, not a fixed schedule. Adjust to your phase, your flight times and your energy.

Day Location Focus
1 Arrive Guangzhou Land, transfer, collect badge if possible, buy a metro card, early night
2 Guangzhou Fair day 1 — breadth pass, collect catalogues, no negotiation
3 Guangzhou Fair day 2 — depth pass with shortlisted exhibitors
4 Guangzhou Fair day 3 — request written quotations, confirm specs
5 Guangzhou Fair day 4 — revisit finalists, sample requests, factory invitations
6 Guangzhou Fair day 5 or a factory visit in Foshan / Zhongshan / Shenzhen
7 Travel to Yiwu Morning high-speed train or flight; evening market orientation walk
8 Yiwu Quote sweep in your primary district
9 Yiwu Negotiation pass, sample purchases, WeChat follow-ups
10 Yiwu Second district or supplier visits; consolidate samples
11 Yiwu / departure city Buffer day, sample courier, move to departure airport
12–13 Departure Fly home, with a buffer for delays

Getting to Yiwu: the counter-intuitive timetable

Most buyers assume the early train is the fast one. On this corridor it is the opposite.

As of November 2026, 12306 lists about five direct trains a day from Guangzhou South to Yiwu. The 06:18 service takes roughly 8h 34m; the 15:09 service takes about 5h 21m. In other words, the afternoon train is roughly three hours faster than the morning one.

For a Day-7 transfer, that changes the plan:

If you leave… You arrive… What the day looks like
06:18 About 14:52 A long travel day, but you reach Yiwu with the late afternoon free for an orientation walk
11:25 or 13:19 18:47 or 20:12 A shorter ride, an evening arrival, dinner and sleep
15:09 About 20:30 The fastest ride, but the day is gone

The early train is still the right choice if you want to see the market on arrival day. The later ones suit buyers who would rather not sit on a train for eight hours and are happy to start fresh the next morning.

Second-class fares cluster around ¥630–770. Book on 12306 with your passport and reconfirm before you travel — times and trains change with each timetable update.

How to use the gap between phases

If you attend a phase that ends before a three-day gap, you have options:

  • Factory visits in Foshan (furniture), Zhongshan (lighting) or Shenzhen (electronics).
  • Travel to Yiwu and start the market early, then return to Guangzhou if you are attending a second phase.
  • Consolidate and ship samples before the second leg, so you are not carrying them.

Trying to attend two phases and visit Yiwu in one trip usually produces a tired buyer with shallow notes. Pick two of the three.

Budget structure, without invented figures

We do not publish prices, because they vary by season, nationality and booking window. Build your own estimate with these categories:

  • International flights (return, or open-jaw into Guangzhou and out of Hangzhou/Shanghai)
  • Domestic transport between cities
  • Accommodation for 10–13 nights, with fair-period premiums in Guangzhou
  • Meals and local transport
  • Samples and their courier costs
  • Badge or registration costs, if any apply
  • Buffer for a changed flight or an extra night

Use the sourcing cost calculator for product-level landed cost, not trip cost — they are different calculations.

Managing samples across two cities

Samples are the heaviest, most annoying part of a two-city trip.

  • Ship from Guangzhou before you leave if you already have enough. Consolidate with a courier or forwarder.
  • Keep one cabin bag for samples you need to compare in Yiwu.
  • Photograph and label every sample with supplier, booth and price before shipping.
  • Record everything in the sample tracking sheet.

Common mistakes

  • Booking the return flight too early on the last day. A delayed train or a slow sample courier can cost you the flight.
  • Assuming Yiwu will have your industrial product. It is a consumer-goods market at heart.
  • Attending two fair phases and Yiwu. Too much movement, too little depth.
  • Not confirming market opening days around public holidays.
  • Leaving sample shipping to the airport. Courier desks at airports are not the place to solve packing problems.

Adapt this itinerary

If you have 7–8 days instead of 12, choose either the fair or Yiwu and do it properly. If you have 15–18 days, add a Foshan or Shenzhen leg and schedule factory visits between phases. The China sourcing itinerary covers the wider multi-city versions, and the Guangzhou to Yiwu route guide covers the transport detail.

Frequently asked questions

Should I visit Yiwu before or after the fair?

After is usually better for a first trip. The fair gives you a price and specification baseline, and Yiwu is easier to evaluate once you know what comparable factory quotes look like.

How many nights in each city?

A common split is five to six nights in Guangzhou for a five-day phase, and three to four nights in Yiwu. Add one night at each end for arrival and departure.

Can I fly home from Yiwu?

Sometimes, depending on your route. Many buyers return to Guangzhou or depart from Hangzhou or Shanghai, which have more international connections. Check both before booking the return leg.

What if my products are only at the fair, not in Yiwu?

Then skip Yiwu. It is a large market for consumer goods and small commodities; it will not replace a factory relationship for industrial products.
