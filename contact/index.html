Contact

Corrections, questions and suggestions

ChinaSourcing is a small independent publication. Email is the only channel, and it is read by the person who writes the guides.

Email

hello@chinasourcinghq.com

We aim to reply within three working days. Corrections with a source usually get a faster response and a public fix with a new verification date.

What you can write about

Corrections

Found a wrong date, a changed market layout or an outdated link? Send the page and, if you have one, the official source. These emails get priority.

Content suggestions

A question our guides do not answer yet, from a buyer who has actually hit the problem, is the most useful kind of suggestion.

Press or partnership

If you run a travel or booking service and want to discuss an affiliate relationship, say which programme you represent — but note that no programme has been joined yet.

What we cannot help with

We are not a sourcing agent, inspection company, freight forwarder, visa consultancy or travel agency. We cannot find suppliers for you, check a factory on your behalf, quote freight, advise on your visa application or intervene in a dispute with a supplier. Emails asking for those services will receive a short reply pointing to the relevant guide instead.

Before you write about a date or a price

Please include the official source you are relying on. Fair dates, market opening hours, visa rules and rail schedules change often, and a correction is only applied when it can be checked against a primary source. Unsourced corrections are noted but not published.
