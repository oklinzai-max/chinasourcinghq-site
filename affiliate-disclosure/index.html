How it works

Some outbound links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and complete a booking or purchase, the partner may pay us a commission. The price you pay is the same as booking directly, unless the partner's own terms say otherwise.

Affiliate links on this site are marked in the page source with rel="sponsored nofollow", and any block containing one carries a short disclosure next to the link itself — not only in this footer page.

Where links appear

Only where they answer a question the page is already addressing. For example, a Canton Fair accommodation guide may offer a hotel search; a guide about travelling between cities may offer a rail search. We aim for a maximum of three affiliate links per guide, and most have fewer.

We do not run a page that exists only to collect affiliate links.

What we have joined — and what we have not

At the time of the last update, no travel affiliate programme had approved this site. That means there are currently no active affiliate links anywhere on ChinaSourcing, and no booking buttons that lead to a partner.

Services we do not promote

Sourcing agents, factory audits, inspection companies, freight forwarders, interpreters, business tour operators and supplier-matching services are outside our first-stage scope. They need individual vetting, so we do not display referral buttons, enquiry forms or “request a quote” widgets for them. Our guides explain what these services do — without pretending we provide them.

Editorial independence

No partner has editorial input. We do not accept payment for a ranking, a review or a mention, and we do not remove criticism of a category because a partner operates in it. If a commission ever influences a recommendation, that would be a breach of the principles on our about page.
