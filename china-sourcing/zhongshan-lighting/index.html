How to source lighting in Zhongshan's Guzhen lighting cluster: what to compare, which technical details decide your landed cost, and how to prepare certification and voltage requirements.

Quick answer

Guzhen, in Zhongshan, concentrates lighting showrooms and component suppliers in a small area. Come with your market's voltage, plug type, certification and packaging requirements written down, and compare the driver, not just the fitting — the driver is where cheap quotes usually hide their savings.

Province
Guangdong
Core area
Guzhen town, Zhongshan
Nearest airports
SZX, CAN, ZUH
Best for
Decorative lighting, LED fixtures, components and drivers

Who this guide is for: Buyers sourcing decorative, commercial, LED or outdoor lighting for retail, projects or distribution.

Why Guzhen exists as a destination

Guzhen is a town in Zhongshan built around one industry: lighting. Showrooms, component suppliers, driver makers and assembly factories sit within a short drive of each other. For a lighting buyer, that density compresses a month of supplier research into a few days.

It is not a single building. It is a cluster of large exhibition halls and individual showrooms, plus factories in the surrounding area.

The fair, and the buildings around it

Guzhen runs on two calendars: permanent showrooms all year, and two lighting fairs each spring and autumn.

The Guzhen lighting fair is the anchor. The 2026 autumn edition (the 35th) ran 22–25 October at the Guzhen Convention and Exhibition Centre, with eight halls spanning smart and commercial lighting, decorative and residential lighting, outdoor and landscape lighting, machinery and components, whole-home smart systems, and new brands. Around it, eight large satellite venues trade at the same time.

Outside fair dates, those buildings operate as permanent showrooms, and the streets around Xingang Road hold hundreds of smaller storefronts and specialist dealers.

The eight satellite venues worth knowing by name: Star Alliance (全球品牌灯饰中心), Huayi Plaza (华艺广场), Wanteng (万腾灯博), Lighting Era Plaza (灯都时代广场), Chang’an Lighting Components City (长安灯配电子城), Caosan International Lighting Components City (曹三国际灯配城), Guyi Ruifeng (古一瑞丰国际灯配城) and Guyi Qingfeng LED (古一庆丰 LED 商贸广场).

Getting there: Guangzhu intercity rail to Guzhen station, then about 10 minutes by taxi. From Guangzhou South it is one train; from Shenzhen, change at Guangzhou South. Fair organisers also run free shuttles between the main venue, the satellite venues and Guzhen station during fair dates.

One day or three days

One day: two or three showroom buildings and a shortlist. Realistic, not comprehensive.

Three days: Day 1 the main exhibition halls and Huayi Plaza for design direction. Day 2 the component markets if you need drivers, chips or housings, plus a second pass at shortlisted showrooms. Day 3 one or two factory visits. Do not attempt five factories in a day — Delta traffic will win.

What to compare before you look at price

Lighting quotes are easy to compare badly, because two fixtures that look identical in a showroom can be entirely different products.

Ask about:

  • LED chip brand and bin. Brand and consistency affect colour and lifetime.
  • Driver brand and specification. The driver is the most common failure point and the most common place to cut cost.
  • Luminous flux and efficacy. Lumens, not “watts equivalent”.
  • Colour temperature and CRI. A 3000K with low CRI looks nothing like a good 3000K.
  • Beam angle and optics quality.
  • Housing material and finish. Aluminium, steel, acrylic, glass — and coating quality.
  • IP rating for outdoor and bathroom products.
  • Dimmability and compatibility with the dimmer systems used in your market.
  • Warranty terms and what they actually cover.

A quote that does not address these is a price for a shape, not for a product.

Voltage, plug and certification

This is where first-time buyers lose money.

  • Voltage: 220–240V for most of Europe, Asia, Australia; 110–120V for North America and parts of Latin America.
  • Plug type: varies by market; a fixture with an integrated plug must match.
  • Certification: CE/UKCA, UL/ETL, SAA, PSE, and others — each with different testing requirements.
  • Driver certification matters as much as the fitting.

Write these requirements into your brief before you travel. Suppliers who export to your market will recognise them immediately; suppliers who do not will quote anyway.

How to work the showrooms

Start with the large exhibition buildings. They concentrate many suppliers and let you see the current design direction quickly.

Then shortlist and go deep. Photograph the model number, the finish name and the business card together. Lighting model names are inconsistent between suppliers, so identification matters.

Ask the awkward questions early:

  • Is this price with or without the driver?
  • Is the LED chip the brand stated on the label?
  • What is the carton size and how many units per carton?
  • What is the MOQ per model and per finish?
  • What is the lead time for a repeat order, not the first order?

Handle fragile goods mentally from day one. Lighting is breakage-prone. Packaging specification affects freight cost and damage claims, and cheap packaging is a false economy.

Sampling lighting properly

  • Order a sample and test it in the configuration you will sell — with your dimmer, your voltage, your installation.
  • Run it for an extended period and check heat at the driver.
  • Compare colour consistency across two units of the same model.
  • Keep one unit sealed as the approved reference.
  • Photograph the label and driver for your records.

Getting there and staying

Guzhen is reached by road or rail from Guangzhou and Shenzhen. Traffic in the Pearl River Delta is unpredictable, so avoid scheduling a tight arrival before a meeting.

Staying in Guzhen itself is the efficient choice for a lighting-focused trip; staying in Zhongshan city centre or nearby Shenzhen adds a daily commute.

Combining with Guangzhou or Shenzhen

Guzhen fits naturally into a Pearl River Delta loop:

  • Guangzhou → Zhongshan → Shenzhen is a common sequence, with high-speed rail between the main cities.
  • If your trip overlaps the Canton Fair, note that lighting products are exhibited in Phase 1.
  • Plan one full day for Guzhen showrooms and a second for factory visits if the order justifies it.

Common mistakes

  • Comparing prices without comparing drivers and chips.
  • Forgetting plug type and voltage until after the order is placed.
  • Assuming certification is included when it is not.
  • Underestimating packaging and breakage costs.
  • Trying to visit five factories in one day in Delta traffic.
  • Buying a design that is already widely copied and competing purely on price.

Frequently asked questions

Is Guzhen only for large orders?

No. Many showrooms quote small trial orders, but MOQ varies widely. Ask per model and per finish — the answer is often different for a bestseller versus a new design.

Can I visit lighting factories in one day?

One or two, realistically. Guzhen showrooms are dense but factories are spread out. If factory visits matter, allocate a separate day and confirm addresses in advance.

What certification do I need?

It depends on your market and product: CE and UKCA in Europe, UL or ETL in North America, SAA in Australia, and others elsewhere. Mains-powered products also need the right plug and voltage configuration.

Why do quotes for the same lamp differ so much?

Usually the driver, the LED chip brand, the housing material and the finishing. Two visually identical fixtures can differ substantially in component quality and lifespan.
