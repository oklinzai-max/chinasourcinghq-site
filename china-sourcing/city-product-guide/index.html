Which Chinese City for Which Products

A practical framework for choosing between Yiwu, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Zhongshan and Foshan based on what you buy, order size, certification needs and how much time you have.

Quick answer

Match the city to the market type, not just the product: Yiwu for small commodities and mixed low-MOQ orders, Guangzhou for breadth and the Canton Fair, Shenzhen for electronics and components, Zhongshan for lighting, Foshan for furniture and building materials. Most products exist in several cities — the right choice depends on your order size and certification needs.

Small commodities
Yiwu — one market, five main districts
General wholesale
Guangzhou — widest category spread
Electronics
Shenzhen — components and contract manufacturing
Lighting
Zhongshan — Guzhen lighting cluster
Furniture & materials
Foshan — Lecong and ceramics

Who this guide is for: Buyers planning a first or second China trip who need to choose one or two cities instead of five.

The honest answer first

Most product categories can be sourced from more than one city in China. Anyone who tells you a product is “only” available in one place is oversimplifying.

What differs is the type of market:

  • Some cities concentrate factories for a product family.
  • Some concentrate traders who aggregate many factories.
  • Some concentrate small-order wholesalers who serve importers, online sellers and retailers.

Your job is to pick the market type that matches your order size and how much verification you need.

A five-question framework

Before you commit to a city, answer these:

1. What is your order size per SKU? Small, mixed, multi-SKU orders point to Yiwu. Container-level single-SKU orders point to factories, often found through the fair or a cluster city.

2. How much customisation do you need? Logo printing is easy anywhere. Custom tooling, new moulds, custom electronics or bespoke packaging needs a factory relationship, not a market booth.

3. What certification does your market require? CE, FCC, RoHS, UKCA, FDA, LFGB, REACH, CPSIA — the list depends on product and destination. Regulated categories need suppliers who already hold test reports. That usually means factories, not trading booths.

4. How much time do you have? Five days supports one city well. Ten days supports two. Three cities in seven days is a transit schedule with shopping attached.

5. Do you need to see production? If yes, choose a city with factories nearby — Shenzhen, Zhongshan, Foshan, and the Guangzhou hinterland all offer this. Yiwu is a trading market first.

City profiles in brief

Yiwu — breadth and small orders

Best for buyers who need many SKUs, low minimums and fast assortment building: accessories, toys, stationery, housewares, socks, seasonal goods, packaging.

Less suitable for: custom electronics, regulated products requiring factory audits, heavy or bulky goods where freight dominates.

Read the Yiwu market guide.

Guangzhou — the generalist

Guangzhou combines the Canton Fair with permanent wholesale markets: apparel, textiles, leather goods, shoes, cosmetics, jewellery, housewares, and more. It is the best single city for a buyer whose categories are unrelated to each other.

It is also the most efficient place to compare factory quotes across many categories in one week.

Read the Guangzhou sourcing guide and the Canton Fair buyer guide.

Shenzhen — electronics and speed

Shenzhen is where components, PCBA, tooling and prototyping live close together. For anything with a circuit board, it is usually the right first stop.

The trade-off: it is a professional sourcing environment, not a browsing market. You need specifications, model numbers and a clear idea of what you want built.

Read the Shenzhen electronics guide.

Zhongshan — lighting

Guzhen, within Zhongshan, is a lighting town: showrooms, component suppliers and assembly factories in a small radius. Buyers sourcing decorative lighting, LED fixtures or components tend to start here.

Read the Zhongshan lighting guide.

Foshan — furniture and materials

Lecong is a furniture market at a scale that takes days to cover, and Foshan also has ceramics and building material clusters. Suitable for project buyers and importers of larger goods.

The trade-off is logistics: furniture freight and damage claims require more planning than small commodities.

Read the Foshan guide.

When to combine cities

Reasonable combinations:

  • Guangzhou + Yiwu — factory quotes plus small commodities. See the route guide.
  • Guangzhou + Foshan + Zhongshan — furniture, lighting and general wholesale within the Pearl River Delta.
  • Guangzhou + Shenzhen — general sourcing plus electronics, connected by high-speed rail in well under an hour.

Unreasonable combinations:

  • Yiwu + Shenzhen + Foshan in five days. You will spend two days moving.
  • Three fair phases plus Yiwu. Choose two priorities.

What the table cannot tell you

  • Whether a specific supplier is reliable. City choice does not replace sampling and verification.
  • What your landed cost will be. Freight, duty and compliance can outweigh a price difference between cities.
  • Whether your product is trending. Market visits show what is available now, not what will sell in your market.
  • Anything about certification compliance. That must be verified product by product.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing a city because a blog said so. Choose based on order size and verification needs.
  • Visiting five cities to “see everything”. You will see airports.
  • Assuming markets and factories are the same thing. A market booth is a sales channel; a factory is a production site.
  • Ignoring certification until after the order. Some suppliers cannot provide the reports you need, and switching later is expensive.

Needs verification before you rely on it

  • — Whether a specific market you plan to visit is open on your dates.
  • — Certification requirements for your product in your destination market.
  • — Current minimum order quantities, which vary by supplier rather than by city.

These points depend on policies, schedules or operators that change. Confirm them on the official source linked below before booking or travelling.

Frequently asked questions

Can I source electronics in Yiwu instead of Shenzhen?

You can find consumer electronics and accessories in Yiwu, especially small items. What Yiwu does not offer is the deep component and contract-manufacturing ecosystem of Shenzhen. For commodity accessories, Yiwu is viable; for a custom device, go to Shenzhen.

Is Guangzhou only useful during the Canton Fair?

No. Guangzhou has large permanent wholesale markets for apparel, leather goods, shoes, cosmetics, jewellery and housewares. The fair is a seasonal layer on top of a year-round market city.

What if my product is not on this list?

Start from the product's supply cluster rather than the city's reputation. Ask suppliers where their competitors are based, and check whether the category has a dedicated trade fair in China — that usually reveals the cluster.

Do I need to visit more than one city?

Only if your categories genuinely sit in different clusters. A single-city trip done properly beats a three-city trip where you spend two days in transit.
