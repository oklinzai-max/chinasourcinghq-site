Why these are free, and what is not here yet

These three documents stay free and ungated: no email, no account, no newsletter. They are the preparation material we would give a friend before their first buying trip.

Two further documents — a supplier comparison spreadsheet and a Chinese-English sourcing glossary — are finished, but not handed out yet: the email delivery that will carry them is still being connected. Until that is live the form below is deliberately not connected either, and it will tell you so rather than pretend otherwise.

How to use them

Print the section you need, not the whole document.

Each section starts on its own page, so you can print two pages for a market day and leave the rest on your laptop. Every document also carries a notes area you can write on.
